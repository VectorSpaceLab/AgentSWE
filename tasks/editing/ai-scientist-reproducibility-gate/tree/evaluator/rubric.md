# Cycle 007 v5 rubric (100-point scale)

The added science safety fence requires the canonical `science_gate` in the transaction receipt to be recomputed from the evidence manifest/policy and the three scientific release bytes; the same digest must appear laterally in the capsule manifest, the attestation, and the notification. Under a high-risk release policy an unsafe decision may not be reported as `release`. When it is missing, drifted, or the decision is inconsistent, the case remains valid but the total is capped at 35; the complete reference control must pass that independent check in all six cases.

v4 additionally adds 8 points for `SCIENCE.EVIDENCE_ATTESTATION`. That assertion is not a string-presence check: the harness independently establishes the sorted reference set from the current evidence manifest, re-reads every safe relative path, computes the declared/observed SHA-256 and the integrity status, and re-establishes every raw metric observation record. The ledger and the report must store exactly the same canonical object and digest; the science gate must also include that binding in its safety-pass condition. Any missing, tampered, duplicated reference, wrong declared digest, or object drift fails the fence and caps the valid case at 35.

v5 deepens that 8-point surface into `SCIENCE.CLAIM_CONTRACT`: the evidence-attestation remains a required input of the contract, but scoring and the safety cap additionally require the actual claim bindings and decision evidence to pass. The evaluator independently constructs the contract from the final artifacts and does not accept a candidate that merely copies the manifest, field names, the expected decision, or self-reported booleans. When the contract is missing, the digest drifts, the semantic check fails, or the transaction digest is inconsistent, the case is valid but the total is capped at 35.

Apply these validity rules before behavioral scoring: a candidate whose patch cannot be applied, cannot launch, times out, or fails the delivery/build preflight receives `0` for that case. Valid but incomplete artifacts receive ordinary partial credit.

Every evaluator-owned checklist totals exactly 100 points. The original science/capsule/budget/transaction, attestation, notification, and entry compatibility total 40 points; the added run provenance journal totals 60 points. The latter must be evidenced by the final parseable artifacts and cross-process behavior, and cannot earn substantive credit through symbol, argument, or file-existence checks.

Capsule credit requires a ZIP containing the exact manifest, the three committed scientific files, and the selected evidence; lexicographically sorted `ZIP_STORED` entries with fixed timestamps/modes; internal SHA-256/size records; a canonical `capsule_id`; policy/project/charge binding; and byte-identical retry recovery. The stage must be integrity-checked before commit. Unselected, symlinked, escaping, missing, or special files are never credited.

Budget credit requires schema-validated usage and policy bytes, authorization, duplicate-call normalization, exact integer ceiling arithmetic, durable project totals, reservation at prepare, one settlement at commit, release on cancel/takeover, policy-version binding, and project-local status/error confidentiality. Race cases require one established simultaneous winner and one structurally refused loser; two failures are not isolation evidence. Cross-surface credit requires capsule publication, scientific publication, and settlement to be one recoverable logical release. Corrupted stages, policy/workspace drift, stale owners, unauthorized participants, and over-budget requests must fail non-zero with a schema-versioned `error.json` while preserving prior bytes and totals. Exact retries recover deleted response artifacts without a second charge or a new commit identity.

The benchmark is invalid only for delivery/apply/build/evaluator defects, malformed fixtures, timeouts, or process-tree RSS violations. Expected product refusals are valid behavioral evidence. Repository/workspace mutation applies only the checklist's 35-point case-local integrity cap and does not remove ordinary partial credit.

Attestation credit requires a project-bound record whose release digest is recomputed from the five release bytes (including the provenance journal), with stable commit/capsule identities, policy version, key identity, and drift or tamper preservation. Outbox credit requires a durable, authorized, project-local event bound to that attestation, and one event/receipt across restarts and lost-response retries.

Run provenance credit is split into five items: the run manifest is bound to the project/script digest; the per-worker seed, experiment, result digest, and failure classification are complete; complete/partial/blocked is decided by policy, and fatal pool/integrity failures are refused before reservation; cleanup is limited to run/session and explicitly protects unrelated processes; the durable journal keeps identical bytes across stage, commit, and lost-response retry. Absolute paths, secrets, unselected files, or peer projects must not leak. A recoverable worker failure is product behavior and should not be penalized for the non-zero worker status itself; failing to block a fatal failure according to policy deducts the corresponding provenance and cross-surface points.
