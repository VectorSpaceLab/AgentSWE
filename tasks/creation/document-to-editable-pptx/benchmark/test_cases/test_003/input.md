# Presentation Request: Key Bridge Incident Evidence and Policy Briefing

Create a 9-slide, 16:9 public-data briefing for a Maryland General Assembly transportation oversight hearing about the 2024 Francis Scott Key Bridge collapse. The deck must separate incident facts from policy choices and must use an evidence cutoff of **2025-03-20**: do not use facts first published after that date.

## Evidence Boundary and Priority

Research public primary sources. Prioritize:

1. NTSB investigation records, preliminary reports, investigative updates, and public docket material available by the cutoff.
2. U.S. Coast Guard and other federal incident-response records.
3. FHWA and Maryland transportation records for closure, recovery, funding, and replacement status.
4. Official testimony and public data.
5. Reputable reporting only for clearly labeled context or to locate a primary document.

An investigative docket or map may require JavaScript or may block scraping. If so, record the limitation and recover through downloadable official reports, attachments, agency releases, or redundant official records. Do not infer probable cause, fault, or regulatory noncompliance from a preliminary record. Preserve uncertainty and the cutoff date.

## Required Content

1. Open with verified human, transport, and infrastructure impact as known by the cutoff.
2. An editable incident timeline from departure through channel reopening, using exact timestamps only where officially supported.
3. A clear not-to-scale site and sequence diagram based on official geography or diagrams; distinguish source-derived geometry from explanation.
4. **Verified event**, **contributing conditions under investigation**, and **unknown as of cutoff** in three explicit columns.
5. Publicly reported response and recovery milestones, including which agency owns each milestone.
6. One native editable chart using a relevant official time series or categorical public dataset; explain why that chart answers an oversight question.
7. Three policy options within state oversight authority, each with evidence basis, implementation dependency, and uncertainty. Do not claim effectiveness that has not been evaluated.
8. A transparent evidence-gaps slide: missing data, conflicting timestamps, access limitations, and what would resolve each gap.
9. Four hearing questions addressed to named institutional roles, not individuals, and a neutral close.

## Design, Notes, and Accessibility

- Use a calm civic system: white, charcoal, federal blue `#1F5A94`, safety orange `#E87522`, and sea green `#2B7A78`. Red is reserved for fatalities or the collision interval.
- Do not use disaster photography as a background. One properly attributed official image may be used when it materially clarifies the site or damage.
- Use native editable timeline, chart, table, and policy-option structures.
- Put concise source and uncertainty speaker notes on slides 2 through 8.
- Cite factual statements and visuals on-slide using `[S#]` and map them to full records in `source_manifest.json`.
- Add meaningful alt text; use labels and patterns alongside color; keep body text at least 18 pt and sources at least 9 pt.
- Use a 0.55-inch margin and a persistent top-right label reading `Evidence cutoff: 20 Mar 2025` on slides 2 through 9.
- Do not identify an individual as the cause, estimate economic loss without an official method, or mix post-cutoff findings into the briefing.

The deck, source manifest, and run report must satisfy the global delivery contract.
