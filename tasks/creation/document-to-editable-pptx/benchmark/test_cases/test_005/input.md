# Presentation Request: Trilingual Aster Field Launch Training

Create an 8-slide, 16:9 launch-and-training deck for distributor trainers introducing **Aster Field**, a fictional offline inspection application. The projected deck will be used in France, Morocco, and the United Kingdom. Learner-facing content must appear in **English, French, and Modern Standard Arabic**, with equal visual prominence and correct right-to-left Arabic layout.

This is a closed-corpus case. Use only:

- [launch_facts.md](assets/launch_facts.md)
- [approved_copy.csv](assets/approved_copy.csv)
- [rollout.csv](assets/rollout.csv)
- [aster_mark.svg](assets/aster_mark.svg)

The optional product screenshots mentioned during planning were not supplied. They are not required. Recover with an honest editable interface or workflow schematic; do not invent a screenshot or show a raster mock interface as though it were the product.

## Language Rules

- `approved_copy.csv` is authoritative for approved headings, UI labels, safety messages, claims, and claims-to-avoid. Preserve every character, accent, Arabic punctuation mark, and product name exactly.
- Present languages in the order English, French, Arabic. Arabic text must be right-aligned and use correct RTL direction; do not reverse character order manually.
- Do not independently translate new learner-facing capability or safety claims. Facilitator notes may be English only.
- Do not use flags to identify languages.

## Required Slides

1. Launch promise and intended users.
2. Before/after inspection workflow as an editable process; show capability boundaries.
3. Offline capture workflow with the exact four approved UI labels.
4. Sync states and the approved safety message; distinguish device-local from server-confirmed states without inventing technical architecture.
5. Native editable table: **Good fit / Not a fit / Escalate**.
6. Rollout by cohort and date as an editable timeline plus a native chart of licensed seats; preserve all rollout values.
7. Trainer practice: two scenarios and a three-step teach-back activity.
8. Readiness check and the exact approved closing instruction.

## Notes, Accessibility, and Brand

- Add English facilitator notes on slides 2 through 8. Notes must include timing, the teaching point, and an answer/correction for each practice item.
- Add alt text in English to every chart, table, diagram, and meaningful visual; include that Arabic appears right-to-left where relevant.
- Use the mark at its original proportions on slides 1 and 8 only.
- Canvas: white. Colors: carbon `#202326`, signal green `#1E9E66`, bright yellow `#F4CD32`, and cool gray `#E9ECEF`. Yellow is reserved for pending or caution states.
- Every content slide must use three equal-width language columns on a shared baseline grid, with a 0.45-inch outer margin and at least 15 pt learner-facing type. Source markers may be 9 pt.
- Use icons or editable state shapes in addition to color. Do not use rounded text pills, decorative gradients, stock photos, or generated device imagery.
- Put compact `[S#]` source markers on factual slides and map every claim, rollout figure, and supplied asset in `source_manifest.json`.
- Make zero search or web-retrieval calls and report those counts truthfully as zero.

The deck, source manifest, and run report must satisfy the global delivery contract.
