# Formal Measurement Rules and Source Priority

## Authority

For this review, these definitions override informal dashboard or memo wording. Monthly values come from `pilot_monthly.csv`. The memo may explain context but may not replace measured values.

## Definitions

- **Successful-session rate** = successful sessions / attempted sessions.
- **Formal uptime** = (available hours - downtime hours) / available hours. Available hours already represent the full calendar observation window for the installed pilot system; do not subtract downtime from the denominator.
- **Cost per successful session** = total cost in the selected period / successful sessions in the same period.
- **First three months** = January through March 2025 inclusive.
- **Last three months** = October through December 2025 inclusive.
- **Percentage change in cost per successful session** = (last-period value - first-period value) / first-period value.

Aggregate rates must be computed from summed numerators and denominators, not by averaging monthly percentages. Display rates to one decimal percentage point and currency to the nearest dollar. Preserve unrounded values in the source manifest calculation records.

## Interpretation Boundary

The data are operational observations from one depot without a control group. They support trend and process statements, not attribution of improvement to a single intervention or a citywide performance forecast.
