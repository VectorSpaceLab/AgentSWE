Tidepool humidity firmware release 2.3

For devices H-7 and H-8 after the 2.3 activation time, corrected humidity equals logged humidity minus 2 percentage points.

An alarm interval begins at the first corrected reading at or above 80 percent and ends at the timestamp of the first corrected reading below 80 percent.

Treat consecutive five-minute samples as a continuous interval; report elapsed minutes, not the number of samples.

Until the next panel reboot, the physical alarm lights continue to evaluate uncorrected logged humidity; the corrected dashboard uses release-2.3 values.

Release 2.3 does not diagnose battery condition or establish the physical cause of an alarm.

Tidepool salinity firmware 2.3-S uses a different correction and is unrelated.
