# Research Request: Current NIH and NSF Public-Access Compliance Brief

Write a 1,800-2,300 word Markdown compliance-planning report for a university research office supporting investigators who expect to submit proposals to both NIH and NSF in August 2026. Compare the controlling public-access requirements as they stood on **2026-06-30**. This is an operational planning document, not legal advice.

Older agency pages and university guides remain highly ranked in search. Distinguish current rules from superseded policies and future or non-applicable changes. Do not merge publication-access obligations, data-management obligations, and optional repository practices.

## Required Analysis

- Identify the controlling NIH publication-access policy, its effective/applicability dates, deposit and availability timing, covered manuscripts, license-rights expectations, and named compliance route. Contrast it with the prior NIH policy and explain exactly which investigators or awards the transition affects.
- Identify the current NSF publication-access and public-access/data-management requirements relevant to an August 2026 proposal and ensuing award. Distinguish agency-wide plans, the operative proposal guide, repository instructions, and discipline- or directorate-specific variation.
- Build a side-by-side workflow matrix from proposal preparation through manuscript acceptance, deposit, public availability, data planning, and record retention. Mark items that require local confirmation.
- Analyze at least three apparent conflicts or stale statements found during research. For each, identify publication date, effective date, supersession relationship, and why a researcher might be misled.
- Cross-check official policy text against current official FAQs or implementation pages and at least two independent university-library or research-office guides. Identify when independent guidance merely repeats the same agency source.
- End with a unified intake checklist, an escalation rule for edge cases, and a list of claims the office should re-verify immediately before the August submissions.

## Evidence Depth

Inspect at least **10 distinct page or document bodies** from at least **5 publishers**, including both agencies, at least two controlling or implementation PDFs, current agency web guidance, the superseded NIH policy itself, and two independent institutional guides. At least seven sources must be `full_page`, `browser_rendered`, or `pdf_full`. Search snippets cannot support report claims. Use refined date/version queries and relevant-link traversal rather than trusting the first policy summary.

## Report Format

Lead with a concise `What applies on 2026-06-30` section. Include a dated policy-lineage table, the workflow matrix, a `Stale guidance traps` section, and the operational checklist. Cite every material rule and date using `[C#]`. The evidence graph must show support and contradiction for stale/current claims and group dependent guidance. Preserve uncertainty where agency scope or award terms require case-specific review.
