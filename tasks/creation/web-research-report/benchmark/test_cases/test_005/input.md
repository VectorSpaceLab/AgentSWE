# Research Request: Should a City Pilot Cool Pavement?

Prepare a 1,900-2,400 word Markdown evidence-transfer report for a hot-climate city considering a one-neighborhood **cool-pavement pilot**. The decision is whether to run a bounded pilot, reject the intervention, or defer pending better evidence. Evaluate public evidence available through **2026-06-30**.

The city cares about pedestrian thermal comfort, near-surface air temperature, pavement surface temperature, nighttime heat, glare, lifecycle and maintenance, and distributional effects. It does not want a recommendation based only on albedo or daytime surface temperature. Do not assume evidence from roofs, parking lots, roads, and pedestrian spaces is interchangeable.

## Required Research

- Define the intervention variants included under `cool pavement` and identify which outcomes and spatial/temporal scales each source actually measures.
- Inspect official pilot reports and underlying technical appendices from at least two cities or public agencies, plus peer-reviewed field or modeling studies and at least one systematic or critical synthesis.
- Compare results across material, climate, season, surface, measurement height, time of day, comparator, and follow-up duration. Extract quantitative effects only when definitions and units are clear.
- Investigate at least three conflicts, including the difference between lower surface temperature and pedestrian radiant exposure or air temperature. Check whether apparently independent reports share the same pilot dataset or authors.
- Assess external validity for a generic hot-climate neighborhood without inventing local geometry or weather. Separate broadly transferable mechanisms from site-specific effect sizes.
- Recommend one of `bounded pilot`, `reject`, or `defer`. If piloting, specify treatment/control segments, pre-period, measurement heights, daytime/nighttime schedule, weather covariates, glare and comfort measures, maintenance observations, equity safeguards, and a decision rule. Do not promise citywide cooling.

## Evidence Depth

Inspect at least **11 distinct source bodies** from at least **7 publishers**, including at least three official public-agency bodies, four full papers or technical PDFs, and two independent syntheses or critical analyses. At least eight must be full-body or full-PDF evidence. Search for null and adverse findings as well as benefits; search snippets do not count.

## Report Format

Lead with the decision and confidence. Include an intervention taxonomy, evidence-comparability table, quantitative effects with units and context, conflict/source-dependence analysis, transferability assessment, and pilot design or deferral rationale. Use `[C#]` citations. Add calculation records for any converted temperature units or synthesized ranges; never average incomparable effects merely to create one headline number.
