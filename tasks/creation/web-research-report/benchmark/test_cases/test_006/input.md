# Research Request: Is Llama 3.1 Acceptable Under an Open-Source-Only Rule?

Write a 1,700-2,200 word Markdown licensing and governance research report for a university AI lab. Its infrastructure policy allows locally deployed model software only when the specific artifact and all required runtime components are under licenses the university has classified as open source. The lab has requested **“Meta Llama 3.1 70B Instruct, the open-source Llama model.”** Determine whether that description is supportable and whether the lab can approve, conditionally review, or must reject the request under the stated rule as of **2026-06-30**.

This is an evidence-based internal policy analysis, not legal advice. Resolve names and artifacts precisely: `Llama`, `Llama 3.1`, the 70B Instruct weights, reference code, documentation, hosted services, and third-party quantizations are not automatically one licensed object.

## Required Research

- Resolve the responsible legal/publishing entity and the exact official repository, model-card, acceptable-use policy, and license text applicable to the requested weights as of the cutoff. Track changed or archived license versions where relevant.
- Identify all material grant, attribution, acceptable-use, redistribution, naming, scale-threshold, and downstream-use clauses. Quote narrowly and avoid giving legal advice.
- Apply the Open Source Initiative's published Open Source Definition and its current position or process concerning open-weight AI licenses. Distinguish `open source`, `open weights`, `source available`, and marketing usage.
- Trace at least four claims calling Llama 3.1 open source or not open source. Determine whether the sources independently analyze the license, quote one another, rely on the same announcement, or refer to a different Llama version.
- Compare the requested weights with the license of the official reference code and at least one necessary runtime or inference component. Do not let an Apache-licensed code repository establish the weight license.
- Identify unresolved ambiguity and what university counsel or procurement must verify. Recommend `approve`, `conditional review`, or `reject under the current rule`, with conditions tied to evidence.

## Evidence Depth

Inspect at least **10 distinct source bodies** from at least **6 publishers or repository owners**. Include the exact official license and model card, official repository files and release/tag evidence, OSI definition/process materials, at least one full legal or scholarly PDF, and multiple independent analyses. At least seven sources must be full-body or full-PDF evidence. Search snippets cannot support a material license claim.

## Report Format

Lead with an artifact-specific answer and confidence. Include an entity/artifact map, clause table with exact locators, terminology comparison, claim-provenance analysis, runtime/license dependency table, and approval decision. Cite claims with `[C#]`. Use `source_relations` to expose copied or version-mismatched claims, and mark superseded license pages explicitly. Accept uncertainty where interpretation requires counsel, but do not replace textual analysis with a generic disclaimer.
