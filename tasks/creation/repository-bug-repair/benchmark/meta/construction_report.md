## Feasibility and limitations

The repositories are large enough to create multiple plausible fault locations but remain standard-library projects with short test runtimes. Construction repairs completed well within the 600-second/4-GiB envelope, including the performance case. The task remains dependent on the created agent's ability to edit and reason across files; no network is needed.

Known limitations are hardware-sensitive timing, scheduler variability beyond synchronized probes, synthetic rather than production-scale repositories, and the need for surrounding OS isolation. Recovery probes cover representative torn/interrupted/retry/rollback states and cannot enumerate all operating-system failure behavior.
